# popdesk 0.6.0

Searches of one or two characters use an index, and two fixes for installations that receive email or keep a long audit log. Everything else is as in [0.5.1](0.5.1.md).

This version is for trying popdesk out. It is not ready for production.

## Upgrading

**Back up before you upgrade** (`popdesk backup <file>`). Migration 26 runs when you start 0.6.0 and cannot be undone: 0.5.x does not start on the upgraded database. From 0.5.0 or 0.5.1, take `compose.yml` from this release and start it. From 0.4.0, read [Upgrading to 0.5.0](../operate/upgrade.md#upgrading-to-050) first. No setting was added or changed.

Migration 26 builds a new search index before the app answers. With 100,000 issues of about 2,300 characters each it took 8 minutes 31 seconds on a development machine, and the index took 395 MB. Do not stop the app while it runs, and with many issues raise the health check's `start_period` first. `popdesk restore` builds the index again, so a restore takes about as long more ([Upgrading to 0.6.0](../operate/upgrade.md#upgrading-to-060)).

## Fixed

- A search text of one or two characters no longer reads every issue. The new index holds the one- and two-character pieces of each issue's title and content. With 100,000 issues, a two-character text that 10 issues hold took 0.4 ms in the database instead of about 5 s, one that 20,000 hold 89 ms, and a character every issue holds 0.33 s. Results are the same as before, in both apps. Filing or editing an issue costs about 8 ms more in the database, and now and then about half a second.
- Receiving email: when the IMAP server answered popdesk's request for one email's content with `NO`, popdesk took what it had so far as the whole email. With nothing yet, the email was discarded and moved to the processed folder; for an email over 1 MB whose second part was refused, an issue or a comment was made from the cut-off text. With `BAD`, or when the header could not be read, every round failed on that email. Now popdesk leaves such an email in the incoming folder, goes on with the next one, and quarantines it after 5 rounds with the reason `UNREADABLE` ([Receiving email](../operate/configuration.md#receiving-email)). If you received email with 0.5.x, look in the processed folder for emails without an issue or with a short one.
- The audit retention job deletes old entries 1,000 at a time, each batch in its own transaction. Requests that write an audit entry wait for one batch, not for the whole run, and an anonymization waits for one batch instead of failing with 409.

## Known problems

As in [0.5.0](0.5.0.md#known-problems), with these changes.

Gone:

- A search text of one or two characters cannot use the index and reads every issue.
- The first retention run after the upgrade deletes all entries past the period in one statement.
- One email the mail server cannot hand over in full fails the whole round before it is counted.

New, in receiving email:

- An email without a `Message-ID` that the server would not give for a while and then gives keeps a record without an outcome; it shows among the emails left to try again for 90 days. An email without a `Message-ID` that was handled but not moved, and that the server then will not give, is quarantined as `UNREADABLE`; its issue is not made twice.
- An email of which the server gives neither the content nor the header is counted in memory: a restart counts its 5 rounds again, and its quarantine does not keep `MAIL_IN_QUARANTINED` up for 24 hours as other quarantines do.
