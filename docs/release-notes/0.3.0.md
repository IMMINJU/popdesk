# popdesk 0.3.0

Customer managers and working on issues: a customer who manages their company's people in the portal, and for issues the filters, search, editing, assignee, status changes, closing and reopening, history, and filing on behalf of a customer.

This version is for trying popdesk out. It is not ready for production.

**Back up before you upgrade** (`popdesk backup <file>`). Migrations 8 to 11 cannot be undone: after the upgrade the 0.2.0 image does not start on the database, and going back means restoring that backup ([upgrade.md](../operate/upgrade.md#upgrading-to-030)).

## The image is not published yet

`deploy/compose.yml` points at `ghcr.io/imminju/popdesk:0.3.0`, and no registry has that image. Build it from this tag under the same name, then start Compose:

```
git checkout v0.3.0
docker build -t ghcr.io/imminju/popdesk:0.3.0 .
docker compose -f deploy/compose.yml up -d
```

Only `amd64` was built and tested. Publishing `amd64` and `arm64` images, and the SBOM, comes with a later release.

## What is new

Customer managers:

- An admin marks customers of a company as its managers on the console. A company can have several; the mark belongs to one company and goes away when the customer leaves it.
- A manager sees the company's people in the portal, invites a new person by email, sends an invitation again, and removes a person from the company. A manager sees the same issues as before.
- When a manager invites an email that already has an account, nothing is sent and the account does not change: a membership request waits for an admin, who approves or rejects it on the console. The manager cannot tell the two cases apart.
- A manager can send 20 invitations a minute (sending again counts), and a company can have at most 100 people who have not accepted yet.

Issues:

- Filters on the issue lists: status, company, and, on the console, assignee and reporter. The console list has the views "mine", "unassigned", and "all", and keeps its filters in the address. So does the console's list of people.
- Search in the title and the content of issues, as the content is shown: Markdown marks and the addresses of links are not searched. An account can send 60 searches a minute.
- Editing the title, content, and priority of an issue, by its reporter or by staff. An edit carries the `version` that was read; when someone else changed the issue in between, nothing is saved and the screen shows the current values.
- Assignee: staff set, change, and clear it. Candidates are the active admins and the active agents assigned to the company. An issue whose assignee was suspended or taken off the company is marked "assignee cannot see it" and has its own filter.
- When an admin takes an agent off a company or suspends an account, the dialog shows how many open issues that person is assigned to and offers to clear them.
- Status changes by staff (`RECEIVED`, `CONFIRMED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `RESOLVED`, `CLOSED`), with the time an issue was resolved and closed.
- The reporter closes an issue at any time, or confirms a resolved one, which closes it. The reporter and staff reopen a resolved issue, or a closed one within 14 days.
- History on the console: who changed the status, assignee, title, content, or priority, when, and from what to what. Customers do not see it.
- Filing on behalf: staff file an issue for a customer who phoned or wrote elsewhere. The customer gets one email with a link and sees the issue as their own; the portal says only that the support team filed it, never who.
- A filing sent twice makes one issue: the portal and the console send an `Idempotency-Key` with each new issue.
- Four more webhook messages: an issue was resolved, closed, reopened, or its assignee changed.

Labels, due dates, internal tasks, the inbox screens, and OIDC are not in this version. Nobody gets an email or an inbox item yet when an issue's status or assignee changes.

## Upgrading from 0.2.0

Read [upgrade.md](../operate/upgrade.md#upgrading-to-030) first. In short:

- **Migrations 8 to 11 only move forward.** Migration 8 adds customer managers and membership requests. Migration 9 adds the assignee, history, and filing keys, and one history entry for every issue that is there. Migration 10 stores the text the search looks in. Migration 11 builds the search index.
- **The database needs the `pg_trgm` extension.** With the Compose file's database nothing has to be done. With your own or a managed PostgreSQL, the app's database user must be allowed to create the extension, or an administrator runs `CREATE EXTENSION pg_trgm;` in popdesk's database before the upgrade. Otherwise popdesk does not start and logs a line that names the statement and the database; nothing of migration 9 is kept, so create the extension and start again.
- **Migrations 10 and 11 can take a while.** Measured on a development machine with 100,000 issues of about 2,000 characters each: migration 9 took 1.5 seconds, migration 10 took 13 seconds, and migration 11 took 51 seconds, 78 seconds from start to healthy. Health checks cannot connect meanwhile. With some hundreds of thousands of issues, raise the health check's `start_period` first.
- **The database grows.** In that measurement the issue table with its indexes went from 279 MB to 722 MB, and the whole database from 289 MB to 748 MB. Make sure the disk has room for about twice the size of the issue table more.
- **Nobody is a manager after the upgrade.** An admin marks managers on the console.
- **A database whose `LC_CTYPE` is `C`** finds Korean and other non-ASCII text without the index, so such a search gets slow as issues add up. The results are the same ([configuration.md](../operate/configuration.md#a-database-you-run-yourself)).
- **Search text is in the address** (`?q=...`), so the access log of your proxy records what people search for. popdesk itself logs no search text. Leave the query string out of that log, or keep the log like other personal data.

## Settings

No key was added or changed. The Compose file differs from the 0.2.0 one in the image tag only.

Other changes an operator can notice:

- A new hourly cleanup job, `idempotency-key-cleanup`, deletes filing keys older than 24 hours. It shows in the cleanup metrics under that `job` label.
- `popdesk restore` no longer restores comments on database objects. So a restore also works where an administrator created the `pg_trgm` extension and the app's user does not own it. The extension still has to be there, or the user has to be able to create it.
- A dump taken with `popdesk backup` from this version on names the `pg_trgm` extension.
- Audit entries of the kinds `AGENT_UNASSIGNED` and `ACCOUNT_SUSPENDED` can carry `clearedIssues`, the number of issues whose assignee was cleared. A receiver of the audit copy sees the new value. Customer managers and membership requests add new kinds of audit entries.
- The webhook sends four more kinds of message: `ISSUE_RESOLVED`, `ISSUE_CLOSED`, `ISSUE_REOPENED`, and `ASSIGNEE_CHANGED`. An issue filed on behalf sends `ISSUE_CREATED` like any other.
- A new dependency in the image: `org.commonmark:commonmark` 0.30.0 (BSD-2-Clause).

## API

The contract files are [openapi-portal.json](../integrate/openapi-portal.json) and [openapi-console.json](../integrate/openapi-console.json); the rules are in [conventions.md](../integrate/conventions.md).

- **The console's issue list answers 400 `UNKNOWN_PARAMETER` for a query parameter the contract does not name.** It ignored such parameters before. The portal's list already did this.
- The account list can answer 429 when it is called with `q`: lists with a search term count together, 60 a minute for an account (the issue lists, the account list, and the list of customers staff can file for).
- The two requests that file an issue take an optional `Idempotency-Key` header. For 24 hours the same key with the same content answers with the first issue; with other content it is 409 `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED`. A request sent again counts toward the limit on creating issues.
- New error codes for issues: `INVALID_TRANSITION`, `NOT_REOPENABLE`, `VERSION_CONFLICT`, `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED`. For customer managers and membership requests: `MEMBER_IS_MANAGER`, `INVITATION_NOT_RESENDABLE`, `NOT_A_CUSTOMER`, `ALREADY_LISTED`, `TOO_MANY_PENDING`, `REQUEST_CLOSED`.
- Suspending an account and taking an agent off a company take `clearAssignments`. The answer to a suspension always has `cleared.issues`.
- An agent can read the names and email addresses of the active customers of a company they are assigned to (`.../reporters`), to choose whom to file for.

## Known problems

The six known problems of 0.2.0 are still there:

- The screens are in Korean only. An account's language changes its emails, not the screens.
- `POPDESK_SANDBOX` does not hold back email, incoming email, or the webhook yet.
- An audit copy that failed for 24 hours cannot be sent again. Export the entries of that time and load them into the receiver.
- The first retention run after the upgrade deletes all entries past the period in one statement.
- Accepting an invitation and setting a new password from a reset link wait for their turn at the password hash while they hold a database connection. With very many of them at once, other requests can wait for a connection.
- Without `SMTP_HOST`, a password reset request is accepted and no email goes out. An admin's password reset shows the link on the screen once in that case.

New in this version:

- A search text of one or two characters cannot use the index and reads every issue: about 2 seconds with 100,000 issues.
- An issue list without a search text takes about 0.3 seconds with 100,000 issues.
- A change of an issue's status or assignee sends no email and no inbox item yet. A membership request sends no email to admins either.
