# popdesk 0.5.0

Everything since 0.4.0: images in issues and comments, attachment files in a folder or an S3 bucket, labels, due dates, tasks and links on the console, the trash, statistics, SLA, branding, tools for personal data, OIDC sign-in, receiving email, and every screen in English as well as Korean.

This version is for trying popdesk out. It is not ready for production.

**Back up before you upgrade** (`popdesk backup <file>`). Migrations 13 to 25 cannot be undone: after the upgrade the 0.4.0 image does not start on the database, and going back means restoring that backup ([upgrade.md](../operate/upgrade.md#upgrading-to-050)). With attachments on `disk` or in `s3`, back those up after the database.

## The image is not published yet

`deploy/compose.yml` points at `ghcr.io/imminju/popdesk:0.5.0`, and no registry has that image. Build it from this tag under the same name, then start Compose:

```
git checkout v0.5.0
docker build -t ghcr.io/imminju/popdesk:0.5.0 .
docker compose -f deploy/compose.yml up -d
```

Only `amd64` was built and tested. Publishing `amd64` and `arm64` images, and the SBOM, comes with a later release.

## What is new

Images in issues and comments:

- The editor of both apps takes images: with the image button, by pasting, or by dragging a file in. The image is uploaded at once and `![file name](attachment:{id})` goes in at the cursor. The save button waits until the upload is done. The preview shows the image before it is saved.
- This works in a new issue, an issue filed on behalf, an edit of an issue, a new comment, and an edit of a comment.
- PNG, JPEG, GIF, and WebP only, judged by the first bytes of the file, not by its name. At most 10 MB for one image.
- An image the author has not saved yet is a temporary image: only that person can see it, not an admin either. A temporary image not saved within 24 hours is deleted without a trace. One person has at most 20 temporary images and 50 MB of them at a time. They count toward the team's total limit like other files.
- When the upload fails, the content does not change and the editor says why under it. When a save fails because an image is over 24 hours old, the content stays and the screen says to upload the image again.
- A saved image is an attachment of the issue. It shows in the list of attachments, marked as a content image, counts toward the 50 MB of the issue unless it is on an internal comment, and is deleted by its uploader or an admin like other files. A customer does not see the images of an internal comment.
- Taking an image out of the content does not delete it. Deleting a comment deletes its images with its other files.
- Images are drawn inside the width of the text, also at 360 pixels. An image that cannot be shown (deleted, or not for this reader) shows its description and a short note in place of a broken picture.
- Only images written as `attachment:{id}` are shown. Image syntax with an `http`, `https`, or `data:` address, and raw `<img>`, stay text, so opening an issue fetches nothing from another server.
- On the console the issue history shows each attachment that was added or deleted, with who and when. Files that come with a new issue are part of "created" and add no line. These history lines send no email, inbox item, or webhook message.

Attachment storage ([configuration.md](../operate/configuration.md#attachment-storage)):

- Files can now be stored in a folder (`POPDESK_STORAGE=disk`) or in an S3 bucket (`POPDESK_STORAGE=s3`), as well as in the database. Until now popdesk did not start with either value.
- Changing the storage moves no file. New files go to the new place, and old files are read and deleted where they are. There is no tool to move files from one storage to another.
- A file's path or key is made of the attachment's ID only, never of its name. Every download still goes through the app, with its permission checks.
- On every start popdesk checks the storage and does not start when the settings cannot be used, when files are stored in a place that has no marker file (for example a volume that is not attached), when files are stored in S3 and the S3 keys are not set, or when it cannot write to the storage for new files. The log says why, without the value of a key.
- New command `popdesk attachments check`: it looks at every stored attachment, says by ID which file is missing or has another size, and ends with `0` (all there), `2` (something missing or of another size), or `1` (the check did not finish). It changes nothing and can run while the app runs.

Labels and due dates, on the console only. Customers see none of the labels, due dates, tasks, and links below: not in the portal, its API, its inbox, or email.

- An admin creates, edits, and deletes the labels of a customer company on the company's page: a name of 1 to 30 characters and a color. Names are unique in a company whatever the case. A company has at most 200 labels. No label can be created in an `ARCHIVED` company. Deleting a label takes it off every issue; the dialog shows how many issues have it.
- Admins and agents put labels of the issue's company on an issue and take them off, at most 20 on an issue.
- Staff set and clear the due date of an issue: today or later, in the installation's time zone (`POPDESK_TIMEZONE`). A due date that has passed is marked "past due" in words, not by color only.
- The issue list has columns for labels and the due date, filters by the labels of one company, and sorts by due date. Labels and due dates go into the issue history; creating, editing, and deleting labels go into the audit log.

Tasks:

- Staff add tasks to an issue: a title of up to 100 characters, a status (open or done), an assignee, and a due date. The assignee must be an active admin or an active agent assigned to that company.
- Each task has notes of up to 4,000 characters. popdesk writes changes of a task's title, status, assignee, and due date into its notes; these cannot be edited or deleted. Task work does not go into the issue history and does not change the issue's version.
- A deleted task or note stays in the list as a trace without its content. On a `CLOSED` issue tasks and notes can be read and deleted, not created or edited.
- Limits: 30 tasks on an issue that are not deleted (100 with the deleted ones), 100 notes on a task that are not deleted, and 60 creations and edits of tasks and notes per account in 60 seconds.
- A new task assignee gets an inbox item and an email, unless they assigned themselves. The email has no note text.
- "My tasks", a new screen in the side menu: the caller's own tasks across issues. A task can be marked done from the list. An issue with open tasks shows "tasks n" in the issue list.
- Resolving or closing an issue with open tasks asks once more. Taking an agent off a company or suspending an account also counts and can clear the person's open tasks.

Links between issues:

- Staff link two issues as duplicate or related, also across customer companies, and remove the link. Both issues must be visible to the person. An issue has at most 50 links; a duplicate has one original, and duplicates do not form chains.
- Staff see only the links to issues they can see. Linking and unlinking go into the history of both issues and change neither issue's version.
- When an issue is resolved or closed, the assignee of each issue linked to it gets an inbox item, without email.

The trash:

- An admin deletes an issue on the console, in any status. It moves to the trash, and nobody sees it there, admins included: on both apps it answers as an issue that does not exist. Nothing is sent about it, and messages still waiting to be sent about it are dropped.
- A new console screen for the trash, admins only, lists each deleted issue and when it will be purged. Restoring brings the issue back as it was, with its comments, attachments, tasks, links, labels, and inbox items. A restore that would break the link rules is refused and names the other issues.
- popdesk purges an issue 30 days after it was deleted, checking every hour; an admin can purge it earlier. Purging deletes the title and content, comments, attachments and their files, tasks, links, labels on it, and inbox items. The key, company, status, times, and history stay. Issue numbers are never used again.
- Deleting, restoring, and purging show in the issue's history, and when an admin does them, in the audit log by company code and issue key, never the title.

Closing resolved issues:

- A resolved issue closes by itself 7 days after it was resolved, checked every 10 minutes. popdesk closes it as "System": the reporter gets the same inbox item and email, and the team the same webhook message, as when staff close an issue.
- An issue reopened or moved on in the meantime is not closed, nor is an issue in the trash.

Statistics:

- A new console screen for all staff: one line per customer company with its open, in progress, resolved, and closed issues. A count leads to the issue list filtered by that company and status. An agent sees only the companies assigned to them.
- While SLA is on, each company also has first response and resolution: the median business time taken and the share of goals met, over the last 7, 30, or 90 days.

SLA, on the console only:

- Admins set the policy on a new screen: for each of the four priorities a first response goal and a resolution goal in minutes, business hours for each weekday, and up to 500 holidays, read on the wall clock of `POPDESK_TIMEZONE`. Each save makes a new version. SLA is on while the policy has at least one goal.
- Only issues filed while SLA is on get due times. Each has two clocks that count business time only. The first public comment of staff stops the first response clock; resolving or closing stops both. Reopening goes on where the clock stopped, and the trash pauses it.
- The issue list has an "SLA" column with the time left or past due and a badge "due soon" (past 80% of the goal) or "overdue", and filters and sorts by them. The issue page shows each goal. Customers never see SLA.
- When a goal passes 80%, the assignee gets an inbox item (or the company's agents, or the admins, when there is no one who can take it). When a goal passes its due time, the same people and every active admin get an inbox item and an email, and the webhook gets one message. Each step is alerted once, within about 2 minutes.
- When a policy change makes issues overdue or due soon, every active admin gets one summary item instead of one item per issue.

Branding, on a new console screen for admins:

- A display name of 1 to 50 characters, an accent color, and a logo for the light and the dark theme. Until an admin changes them, the name is `popdesk`, the colors are the apps' own, and there is no logo.
- An accent color is taken only when its text reaches a contrast of 4.5:1 on every background it is shown on, in both themes. The screen shows both themes and their contrast before saving.
- A logo is a PNG, JPEG, or WebP file of up to 512 KB; an SVG is not taken. popdesk scales it to fit 512 by 128 px and keeps only that copy, as a PNG without metadata.
- The branding shows on the sign-in screens, the header of both apps, the browser tab title, and in emails: the sender's name is the display name unless `MAIL_FROM` has a name of its own, and the invitation, password reset, and test emails use the display name. Emails have no logo, and the favicon stays popdesk's.

Personal data ([privacy.md](../operate/privacy.md)):

- An admin exports one account as a JSON file from the member list: the account, the profile photo, the issues the person reported, and the comments and attachment names they wrote. It holds no other person's name, email, or account ID, and no file content.
- An admin anonymizes an account from the same menu, after typing the person's email in. The name becomes the anonymous name in the installation's language, the email `deleted-{id}@invalid`, and the account is suspended and signed out. The profile photo, the password, open invitation and reset tokens, queued emails to the person, their inbox items, and their OIDC link are removed. What the person wrote stays, under the anonymous name. Nothing brings the account back.
- An anonymized account shows as anonymized in the member lists and cannot be made a company's manager.
- An anonymization puts the anonymous values into the person's audit entries and computes the hash chain again from there, recorded in a new entry `AUDIT_RECHAINED` ([audit-log.md](../operate/audit-log.md#after-an-anonymization)). If the chain is already broken in that range, the anonymization is refused and nothing changes. [`audit-verify.py`](../integrate/audit-verify.py) reports such a range as "computed again", not as a break.
- An admin exports one company's data as a ZIP from the company's page ([privacy.md](../operate/privacy.md#exporting-a-companys-data)): its issues, public comments, their files, and the member customers, and on request private issues, internal comments, and tasks. Staff are "support team" everywhere in the ZIP. popdesk writes it in the background, one at a time in the installation, and any admin can download it within 24 hours. The admin who asked is told when it is done; every admin is told when it fails, also when the app stopped while it ran.
- Automatic issue cleanup, off by default ([privacy.md](../operate/privacy.md#automatic-issue-cleanup)): with `POPDESK_ISSUE_RETENTION` set, once a day popdesk moves to the trash every issue closed longer ago than that period, at least 90 days. When it is turned on or its period gets shorter, it moves nothing for 24 hours and tells every admin how many issues it would move, or that none is due yet.

OIDC sign-in ([configuration.md](../operate/configuration.md#oidc-sign-in)):

- With `OIDC_ISSUER`, `OIDC_CLIENT_ID`, and `OIDC_CLIENT_SECRET` set, the sign-in page shows a button for the company account, on the apps that `OIDC_APPS` names. Password sign-in and "Forgot password" stay on.
- OIDC makes no account; people are invited as before. The first OIDC sign-in matches an account by the verified email in the ID token and links it to the person at the provider. After that only the link counts.
- An invited account whose invitation is less than 7 days old becomes active at its first OIDC sign-in. An account locked after five wrong passwords can still sign in with OIDC.
- An admin can remove a link from the member list. One IP address can start 20 OIDC sign-ins a minute.

Receiving email ([configuration.md](../operate/configuration.md#receiving-email)):

- popdesk reads one mailbox over IMAP once a minute, at most 100 emails a round, and turns each email from a customer into a new issue or into a public comment on the issue it answers. It is on when `IMAP_HOST`, `IMAP_USER`, `IMAP_PASSWORD` (or `IMAP_PASSWORD_FILE`), and `MAIL_INBOUND_ADDRESS` are all set, outside a sandbox. Use a mailbox that only popdesk reads.
- Only an `ACTIVE` customer account can send, by the address in its `From`. Email from an unknown address, from staff, from an account that is not active, automatic replies and bounces, and email that fails DMARC are dropped without an answer. The same email is handled once.
- A new issue goes to the customer's company; a customer of several companies adds the company code after a `+`, such as `support+ACME@example.com`. A reply becomes a comment when it points at a popdesk email about the issue, or has the issue key in square brackets in its subject. The quoted text under the reply is cut.
- When popdesk cannot file the email or add the reply, the customer gets a short email that says so, at most one every 15 minutes.
- A handled email moves to `IMAP_PROCESSED_FOLDER`. One that failed 5 times, is too large, or went over the limits moves to `IMAP_QUARANTINE_FOLDER`; take it out by hand. Emails in both folders are deleted after 90 days.
- Password sign-in to IMAP only. Microsoft 365 cannot be used; Google Workspace needs an app password.
- Every email popdesk sends now has a `Message-ID` that popdesk makes and `Auto-Submitted: auto-generated`. While receiving email is on, an email to a customer about an issue has `Reply-To: MAIL_INBOUND_ADDRESS`.

Screens in English:

- Every screen of the console and the portal comes in English as well as Korean. After sign-in the screens follow the account's language and change at once when it is changed in "My info". Before sign-in they follow the browser: Korean when its first language is Korean, else English.
- Numbers and dates follow the screen language.

For the operator, in the console's system status:

- Admins get an inbox item when a cleanup job has not succeeded for its interval plus 24 hours. It comes again every 24 hours while a job is late.
- A "Receiving email" part and an "OIDC sign-in" part: whether each is on, the last success, and the failures with the last one and its kind.
- A cleanup job that fails writes one warning to the log, `Scheduled work '<job>' failed (<kind of error>); it is tried again later.`, and runs again a minute later. The wording stays the same between versions.

Smaller changes:

- A button that cannot be pressed now (waiting for a rate limit, another action running, a confirmation field that is empty) looks like a disabled button.
- After "Reload" on a failed load, the focus goes to the screen's title, or to the dialog's title inside a dialog.
- Opening one item in the notification inbox lowers the unread count in the header at once.
- The audit log's "Action" and "Target" columns stay on one line; on a narrow screen the table scrolls inside its frame.
- The member list of a company fits its frame at 820px; a long email breaks over lines.
- Before the screen is drawn, the browser tab shows "popdesk" on both apps.

Fixed:

- The mail server's answer shown after a test email could keep control characters from U+0080 to U+009F.
- Commenting on an issue while its assignee was being changed could answer 500.
- In the console's collapsed side menu the sign-out button stuck out of the menu.
- Screen readers now get a name for the links in the collapsed side menu and a profile link name that matches the visible text. A table that scrolls sideways can take the keyboard focus.
- On the portal's issue list, an empty space showed above "Only issues I filed" when it went to a line of its own.

A held queue for received email, OAuth 2.0 sign-in to IMAP, putting a quarantined email back from the screen, choosing the statistics period by dates, and statistics per agent are not in this version.

## Upgrading from 0.4.0

Read [upgrade.md](../operate/upgrade.md#upgrading-to-050) first. In short:

- **Migrations 13 to 25 only move forward.** Each runs in its own transaction. They add tables, columns, and indexes for content images (13), labels, due dates, tasks, and links (14 to 16), the trash and closing resolved issues (17), SLA (18 to 20), branding (21), anonymization (22), company exports and automatic issue cleanup (23), OIDC (24), and received email (25). Rows that are there are not changed, except that migration 17 gives every `RESOLVED` issue the time it was resolved as its start for closing by itself, and migration 18 makes SLA policy version 1 without goals. On the upgrade test's sample database (a few issues) all thirteen took 0.3 seconds. On a large database these take longer: 13 checks every attachment row again, 17 writes every `RESOLVED` issue, and 17, 18, 20, and 23 build indexes on the issue table. popdesk answers no request while they run.
- **Check these settings before you upgrade**, because a wrong value now stops popdesk:
  - `POPDESK_TIMEZONE`: up to 0.4.0 a value that was not a time zone quietly became UTC. Now an empty value or anything but an IANA zone name (`Asia/Seoul`) or `UTC`, such as `KST` or `+09:00`, makes popdesk answer 503 `SETUP_REQUIRED`; the health check stays unhealthy. Leaving the setting out still means UTC.
  - `POPDESK_ISSUE_RETENTION`: in the docs since 0.1.0 without effect, read now. A value that is not a duration of days and hours, or one shorter than `P90D`, stops the start. A valid value turns automatic issue cleanup on.
  - `OIDC_*`: read now. Some of `OIDC_ISSUER`, `OIDC_CLIENT_ID`, and `OIDC_CLIENT_SECRET` but not all, an `OIDC_APPS` other than `console`, `portal`, or `both`, or an `OIDC_ISSUER` that is not an absolute `http` or `https` address stops the start.
  - `IMAP_*` and `MAIL_INBOUND_ADDRESS`: read now. Some of the four required ones but not all, an `IMAP_PORT` outside 1 to 65535, a `MAIL_INBOUND_ADDRESS` that is not one address or holds a `+`, or folder settings that are the same stop the start. With all four set, popdesk moves every email in that mailbox.
- **Within 10 minutes after the start, resolved issues older than 7 days are closed**, each with an email to the reporter and a webhook message. Many old resolved issues mean many emails at once, at most 50 an hour to one address; the rest are failed with `RATE_LIMITED` and can be sent again from the system status. On a development machine 20,000 such issues were closed in 39 seconds.
- **A cleanup job that has not succeeded for its interval plus 24 hours raises an inbox item in the first minutes.**
- **The sender's name of every email changes** to the display name (`popdesk` until an admin sets one), unless `MAIL_FROM` already has a name.
- **More email goes out** with `SMTP_HOST` set: task assignments, and once SLA is on, overdue goals.
- **Give company exports room.** The ZIPs go into `POPDESK_EXPORT_DIR`, by default `/tmp/popdesk-exports` in the container, which is lost when the container is replaced. Mount a volume there before the first large export ([configuration.md](../operate/configuration.md#company-exports)).
- **Read [Attachment storage](../operate/configuration.md#attachment-storage) before you change `POPDESK_STORAGE`.** With `disk` or `s3`, `popdesk backup` does not hold the files: back up the folder or the bucket after the database dump, and after a restore run `popdesk attachments check`.
- Nothing has to be set. SLA is off, automatic issue cleanup is off, OIDC and receiving email stay off without their settings, and the branding is the default.

## Settings

| Key | Change |
|---|---|
| `POPDESK_STORAGE` | `disk` and `s3` work now. With a value other than `db`, `disk`, or `s3` popdesk does not start |
| `POPDESK_STORAGE_DIR` | Used with `disk`. Must be an absolute path with a volume mounted there, writable by the user ID `10001` |
| `S3_BUCKET`, `S3_ACCESS_KEY`, `S3_SECRET_KEY` (`_FILE`) | Required with `s3`, and the key pair also while any attachment is still in S3. popdesk takes its credentials from these keys only, never from `AWS_` variables, a profile file, or an instance role. The bucket must exist |
| `S3_ENDPOINT`, `S3_REGION` | One of the two is required with `s3`. With `S3_ENDPOINT` requests go to that address in path style; without it, to AWS in `S3_REGION`. An `http` address gets a warning at start |
| `POPDESK_TIMEZONE` | Stricter: an empty value or anything but an IANA zone name or `UTC` answers 503 `SETUP_REQUIRED`. Also the zone of due dates and of SLA business hours and holidays. Changing it makes a new SLA policy version within a minute of the start; give every instance the same value |
| `POPDESK_LOCALE` | Also chooses the anonymous name an anonymization stores (`Deleted user` for `en`, `삭제된 사용자` for `ko`) |
| `MAIL_FROM` | When it has a name, that name wins over the display name as the sender's name |
| `POPDESK_ISSUE_RETENTION` | Read now: how long after closing an issue it is moved to the trash, an ISO-8601 duration of days and time (`P1095D`), at least `P90D`. Empty is off. Any other value stops the start |
| `POPDESK_EXPORT_DIR` | New: the folder where company exports are written. Default `/tmp/popdesk-exports` |
| `OIDC_ISSUER`, `OIDC_CLIENT_ID`, `OIDC_CLIENT_SECRET` (`_FILE`) | Read now. Set all three or none. `OIDC_ISSUER` must be written exactly as the provider's discovery document writes it |
| `OIDC_APPS` | Read now: `console` (default), `portal`, or `both` |
| `IMAP_HOST`, `IMAP_USER`, `IMAP_PASSWORD` (`_FILE`) | Read now. With `MAIL_INBOUND_ADDRESS` they turn receiving email on, outside a sandbox |
| `IMAP_PORT` | Read now: default `993`, TLS from the start; any other port requires STARTTLS |
| `IMAP_FOLDER`, `IMAP_PROCESSED_FOLDER`, `IMAP_QUARANTINE_FOLDER` | Read now. Defaults `INBOX`, `popdesk-processed`, and `popdesk-quarantine`. The three must differ |
| `IMAP_ALLOW_PLAINTEXT` | Read now: `true` allows an unencrypted connection when the server does not offer STARTTLS, with a warning at startup |
| `MAIL_INBOUND_ADDRESS` | Read now: one address without a `+`. Emails to customers about an issue reply to it |
| `POPDESK_SANDBOX` | Now also turns off receiving email, although the `IMAP_*` settings are there |

The storage, `OIDC_`, `IMAP_`, and `POPDESK_ISSUE_RETENTION` keys have been in [configuration.md](../operate/configuration.md#environment-variables) since 0.1.0; only `POPDESK_EXPORT_DIR` and `MAIL_INBOUND_ADDRESS` are new names. The Compose file differs from the 0.4.0 one in the image tag and in passing 23 more keys to the app when you set them: the six `S3_` keys, `POPDESK_ISSUE_RETENTION`, `POPDESK_EXPORT_DIR`, the five `OIDC_` keys, and the ten keys of receiving email.

Other changes an operator can notice:

- Seven new cleanup jobs: `temporary-image-cleanup`, `trash-purge`, `issue-auto-close`, `sla-recalculate`, `issue-retention`, `company-export-expiry`, and `mail-record-cleanup` ([configuration.md](../operate/configuration.md#sending-email-and-webhook-messages)). They show in the system status and in the cleanup metrics under their `job` label. A receiver of the cleanup metrics that lists the jobs it knows needs these seven.
- New inbox items: for staff `TASK_ASSIGNED`, `LINKED_ISSUE_RESOLVED`, `LINKED_ISSUE_CLOSED`, `SLA_RESPONSE_WARNING`, `SLA_RESPONSE_BREACHED`, `SLA_RESOLUTION_WARNING`, and `SLA_RESOLUTION_BREACHED`; for admins `CLEANUP_STALLED`, `SLA_RECALCULATED`, `COMPANY_EXPORT_READY`, `COMPANY_EXPORT_FAILED`, `ISSUE_RETENTION_ARMED`, `MAIL_IN_STALLED`, and `MAIL_IN_QUARANTINED`.
- New kinds of audit entry: `LABEL_CREATED`, `LABEL_UPDATED`, `LABEL_DELETED`, `ISSUE_DELETED`, `ISSUE_RESTORED`, `ISSUE_PURGED`, `SLA_POLICY_CHANGED`, `BRANDING_CHANGED`, `BRANDING_LOGO_CHANGED`, `ACCOUNT_EXPORTED`, `ACCOUNT_ANONYMIZED`, `AUDIT_RECHAINED`, `COMPANY_EXPORTED`, `COMPANY_EXPORT_DOWNLOADED`, `OIDC_LINKED`, `OIDC_UNLINKED`, and `INVITATION_ACCEPTED`. `SIGN_IN_SUCCEEDED` and `SIGN_IN_FAILED` have `{"method": "OIDC"}` in `changes` for an OIDC sign-in. A receiver of the audit copy or of exports that lists the kinds it knows needs all of them.
- After an anonymization, an outside copy or an older export of the audit log differs from popdesk in the range of the `AUDIT_RECHAINED` entry. That is not a sign of tampering ([audit-log.md](../operate/audit-log.md#after-an-anonymization)).
- The webhook sends two more kinds of message: `SLA_RESPONSE_BREACHED` and `SLA_RESOLUTION_BREACHED`. An issue filed by email sends `ISSUE_CREATED`, and a reply turned into a comment `CUSTOMER_COMMENT`, as from the portal.
- New emails: a task was assigned to you, an SLA goal is overdue (one for first response, one for resolution), and the two short answers to a customer whose email could not be filed or added.
- New metrics: `popdesk_mailin_messages_total` (label `outcome`), `popdesk_mailin_unauthenticated_total`, and `popdesk_mailin_round_failures_total` (label `kind`).
- An email over the hourly limit of 50 is logged at `INFO` now, not `WARN`. The `WARN` line when `DELIVERY_FAILED` is raised, and the metric `popdesk_mail_rate_limited_total`, stay.
- popdesk leaves a marker file the first time it uses a folder or a bucket: `.popdesk-storage` in the folder, `popdesk/.popdesk-storage` in the bucket. Do not remove it.
- A download whose file is missing or cannot be read answers 500 `INTERNAL_ERROR`, with every storage, and the log has the attachment's ID. A download that fails halfway closes the connection.
- Every request to S3 has a time limit: 5 seconds to connect, 60 seconds for an answer.
- Logos are stored in the database, so `popdesk backup` covers them. At most 2 images are decoded at once, profile photos and logos together; a third upload at that moment gets 503 `SERVER_BUSY`.
- When the app starts, a company export that was running becomes failed, and every file in `POPDESK_EXPORT_DIR` that is not the ZIP of a finished export is deleted.
- An `http` OIDC issuer gets a warning at startup. ID tokens are checked against the host's clock with 60 seconds of leeway; keep the clock set by NTP.
- A sandbox with `IMAP_*` settings logs a warning at startup that receiving email is off.
- The audit log does not record `popdesk attachments check`.
- New dependencies in the image: the AWS SDK for Java v2 (`software.amazon.awssdk:s3` and `url-connection-client` 2.55.15); jsoup 1.23.2, which turns the HTML part of a received email into text; and Spring Security's OAuth 2.0 client and JOSE modules (7.1.1) with Nimbus JOSE + JWT 10.9.1 and the Nimbus OAuth 2.0 SDK 11.38.2. jsoup is under the MIT license, `reactive-streams` 1.0.4 (pulled in by the AWS SDK) under MIT-0, and the rest under the Apache License 2.0. The image is 206 MB, 12 MB more than 0.4.0 (uncompressed, as `docker image inspect` gives it).

## API

The contract files are [openapi-portal.json](../integrate/openapi-portal.json) and [openapi-console.json](../integrate/openapi-console.json); the rules are in [conventions.md](../integrate/conventions.md).

Both apps:

- Temporary images: uploading (`uploadImage`) and reading by the uploader (`downloadImage`, `headImage`). The read answers the same 404 whatever the reason. Filing an issue, filing on behalf, editing an issue, and adding and editing a comment take `imageIds`; nothing is saved when one cannot be attached. New error codes 400 `FILE_NOT_IMAGE`, `TEMPORARY_IMAGE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, and `TEMPORARY_IMAGE_INVALID`. An attachment has the new field `inline`. Uploading a temporary image counts toward the 30 uploads a minute.
- Public, without a session ([conventions.md](../integrate/conventions.md#branding)): `getPublicBranding` and `getBrandingLogo`, and `getSignInOptions`. `startOidcSignIn` and `finishOidcSignIn` are pages the browser opens: they answer 302, or 404 on a host whose app does not have OIDC on ([conventions.md](../integrate/conventions.md#oidc-sign-in-auth-6)).
- An issue in the trash, or purged, is 404 on every issue path, to admins too.

The console (the portal's contract has no other change):

- Labels: `listCompanyLabels`, `createLabel`, `updateLabel`, `deleteLabel`, `addIssueLabel`, `removeIssueLabel`. Due dates: `setIssueDueDate`, `clearIssueDueDate`. New error codes 400 `LABEL_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` and `ISSUE_LABEL_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, 409 `LABEL_NAME_TAKEN`.
- Tasks: `listIssueTasks`, `createTask`, `updateTask`, `deleteTask`, `createTaskNote`, `updateTaskNote`, `deleteTaskNote`, and `listMyTasks`. New error codes 400 `TASK_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, `TASK_NOTE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, `TASK_DELETED`, and `TASK_NOTE_DELETED`.
- Links: `listIssueLinks`, `createIssueLink`, `deleteIssueLink`. New error codes 400 `LINK_TO_SELF`, `LINK_DUPLICATE_CHAIN`, and `LINK_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, 409 `LINK_EXISTS`. `ISSUE_CLOSED` now also covers tasks, notes, and links.
- The trash: `deleteIssue`, `listTrash`, `restoreIssue`, `purgeIssue`, admins only. New error code 409 `RESTORE_LINK_CONFLICT` with the new error field `issueKeys`.
- Statistics: `getCompanyStats` (`GET /api/console/stats`), with `days` 7, 30, or 90, and `sla` on each item while SLA is on.
- SLA: `getSlaPolicy` and `updateSlaPolicy`, admins only, with `version` and 409 `VERSION_CONFLICT`. Issues and list items have `sla`. The issue list takes `sla=warning` or `sla=breached` and `sort=slaDue`.
- The issue list also takes `label` and `sort=dueDate`. Issues have `labels`, `dueDate`, and `overdue`; list items have `openTaskCount`. The issue history has the new fields `ATTACHMENT`, `LABEL`, `DUE_DATE`, `LINK`, and `TRASH`.
- Branding: `getBranding`, `updateBranding`, `previewAccent`, `uploadBrandingLogo`, `deleteBrandingLogo`, admins only. New code `LOW_CONTRAST` in `fields`.
- Accounts: `exportAccount`, `headAccountExport`, `anonymizeAccount`, and `unlinkAccountOidc`, admins only. `Account`, `SuspendedAccount`, and the account list have `anonymized` and `oidcLinked`; `CompanyMember` has `anonymized`. New error codes 400 `ACCOUNT_ANONYMIZED` (also for making an anonymized account a company's manager), 409 `ANONYMIZATION_RUNNING`, and 409 `AUDIT_CHAIN_BROKEN` with `brokenSeq`.
- Company exports: `createCompanyExport` (202), `listCompanyExports`, `downloadCompanyExport`, `headCompanyExport`, admins only ([conventions.md](../integrate/conventions.md#company-export-pii-8)). New error code 409 `EXPORT_RUNNING`.
- System status: new fields `oidc` and `mailIn`. Inbox items have `task`, `linkedIssue`, `slaSummary`, `companyCode`, `retentionCount`, and `retentionDays`, and the new kinds listed above. What popdesk itself did has `null` as the actor.
- `SETUP_REQUIRED` (503) also covers a `POPDESK_TIMEZONE` popdesk does not take. A customer calling a console endpoint gets 404 on the portal host and 401 on the console host; the docs said 403 before.

## Known problems

Still there from earlier versions:

- The first retention run after the upgrade deletes all entries past the period in one statement.
- Accepting an invitation and setting a new password from a reset link wait for their turn at the password hash while they hold a database connection. With very many of them at once, other requests can wait for a connection.
- Without `SMTP_HOST`, a password reset request is accepted and no email goes out. An admin's password reset shows the link on the screen once in that case.
- A search text of one or two characters cannot use the index and reads every issue: about 2 seconds with 100,000 issues.
- The hourly limit of 50 emails can let a 51st through when the send loop and a test email go to the same address at the same moment.

Gone in this version: the screens are in English as well as Korean; an issue list without a search text takes about 0.1 seconds for an admin and 0.15 seconds for an agent with 100,000 issues, not 0.3; a sandbox turns off receiving email; admins get an inbox item when a cleanup job has not succeeded for a long time.

New in this version:

Images and attachment storage:

- The limit of 20 temporary images and 50 MB for one person can be passed by one or two when uploads go at the same moment.
- When text in front of an image is typed while the image uploads, in a place where the same characters repeat (such as between two empty lines), the image can land one character off.
- The start check sees only that a folder or a bucket was used by popdesk, not by which installation. A volume or a bucket of another popdesk installation is not caught at start; `popdesk attachments check` finds the missing files.
- With `s3`, popdesk does not start while S3 is down, also for a moment.
- The `s3` storage was tested against an S3-compatible server, not against AWS S3 itself.

Tasks, links, and the trash:

- When two people change the same field of a task at the same moment, the later change stays. Both changes are in the task's notes.
- Setting a task's assignee at the moment an admin takes that agent off the company can leave the agent as the assignee. No email goes out to them, and the task is not in their "My tasks". The same holds for the assignee of an issue.
- Inbox items stay after the task is given to someone else or the link is removed. An item about a link is shown while the reader can see both issues.
- The 400 for a duplicate chain or for 50 links can tell an agent that an issue has links to issues they cannot see, not which ones.
- When an issue is linked at the moment the other issue is resolved or closed, the assignee may or may not get the inbox item.
- When an issue is deleted at the moment an email or webhook message about it is being sent, that one message can still go out.
- When the hourly purge keeps failing on one issue, the rest of that run stops too. It shows as a failed cleanup job.
- When popdesk cannot read a cleanup job's last success, it leaves the cleanup alert as it is. If that read keeps failing, the alert is never raised; the job's warning in the log and its last run in the system status remain.

SLA:

- For up to a minute after a policy change, the console shows the due times of the old version, until `sla-recalculate` works them out again.
- During a rolling update, two instances with different values of `POPDESK_TIMEZONE` can each make a policy version for their zone, so the version may go up twice. Give every instance the same value.
- On a morning when many goals run out at once, an admin can reach the limit of 50 emails an hour. The overdue items stay in the inbox, and the issue list with "overdue" shows them all.
- At the one second when the time used equals the goal, the issue page shows the goal as due soon while the alert already counts it as overdue.
- With 100,000 issues, an agent's first page of the issue list sorted by SLA due time takes about 0.2 seconds.

Branding:

- On the portal at 360 px, with a wide logo, the user menu and sign-out move to a second line of the header. Nothing is cut off.

Personal data:

- An anonymization is one request that stays open until the chain is computed again: about 20 seconds for 100,000 audit entries on a development machine. With a large log, raise the proxy's time limit for the console host, as for the integrity check.
- popdesk does not find the person's name or email in other people's issues and comments. Search for them in the issue list and change or delete what you find.
- Data outside the database keeps the old name and email after an anonymization: audit entries already sent to the outside copy, audit log exports, account and company exports already downloaded, emails already sent, and backups. A company ZIP made before keeps them for up to 24 hours.
- popdesk does not measure the free disk space before a company export. A ZIP that does not fit fails with `DISK`.

OIDC sign-in:

- Microsoft Entra ID does not put `email_verified` into its ID tokens, so with it no account can be linked. It is not supported yet. popdesk reads the email from the ID token only, not from the `userinfo` endpoint.
- popdesk trusts the provider to say who holds an email: a provider admin who can give anyone any verified email can sign in as any account that is not linked yet.

Receiving email:

- One email the mail server cannot hand over in full fails the whole round before it is counted. When it is near the front of the folder, every round fails until an admin moves it out of the mailbox; `MAIL_IN_STALLED` shows it after an hour.
- When moving an email by `COPY` is cut off, the processed folder can hold two copies, or the incoming folder an email marked as deleted. No issue is filed twice.
- If the receiving loop stops on an unexpected error, `MAIL_IN_STALLED` is not raised either. Only `lastSuccessAt` in the system status stops moving.
- When the attachment storage is down for over 5 minutes, emails with attachments are quarantined after five rounds. Move them back to the incoming folder by hand.
- On a mail server without `UIDPLUS`, popdesk does not delete the 90-day-old emails in the processed and quarantine folders and logs a warning once a day. Delete them there yourself.
- A domain without DMARC can still be forged. Email forged in a customer's name counts against that customer's limits, so it can hold back their portal filing for a minute.
- The round counts and the last failure in the system status start from zero after a restart.

Screens in English:

- An error code the screen does not know shows the server's message, and the name of an anonymized account is in the installation's language. Date and time fields drawn by the browser follow the browser's own language.
